## What EGGA does

EGGA runs a company's growth, sales and marketing as one service: a clear plan, visible work and progress the owner can track every month. Strategy and execution sit in the same monthly plan, and one dedicated contact keeps priorities, deliverables and reporting tied to the business goal. Agencies often start from deliverables. EGGA starts from the business, agrees the one commercial goal worth chasing, and puts every piece of work against it.

The idea behind EGGA is simple. For decades the sharpest growth, sales and marketing sat behind budgets only large corporations could pay. AI changed the cost of that work. EGGA puts it to work for SME owners who build real businesses, the companies that hire locally and invest where they live. AI supports the research, content, visibility and reporting; human judgment decides what is true, useful and worth saying.

Read more on [how EGGA works](https://egga.ai/how-it-works) and [why EGGA exists](https://egga.ai/about).

## Who EGGA is for

EGGA works with B2B SMEs that are owner-led or founder-led, have a real growth opportunity and have a decision-maker ready to move. The businesses it backs are usually at one of four points:

- **Building on a family legacy.** A second-generation owner taking an established company somewhere new.
- **Outgrowing referrals.** Referrals built the business, and the next stage needs strangers to find it too.
- **Entering a new market.** A new country, a new sector or a new kind of buyer.
- **Preparing to sell or scale.** Buyers and investors pay for growth they can see repeat, and the numbers have to prove it.

EGGA is a good fit for B2B companies with long sales cycles, buying committees and technical buyers, for example manufacturers, distributors, industrial and professional services firms, and B2B technology companies.

## The four pillars of the work

Every EGGA plan covers the same four areas. The plan sets how much work happens each month, not which areas are included.

### 1. Direction: where to grow and who to sell to

In month one EGGA and the owner agree the one commercial goal worth chasing: more revenue from existing clients, a sharper offer, or a new market. Every idea, channel and request is then tested against that goal. What passes gets an owner, a deadline and a 90-day target. The output is a 90-day growth plan, a named ideal customer, a sharpened offer, price and channel mix, and the message that makes the company easy to choose.

### 2. Visibility: found on Google, in AI answers and on the website

B2B buyers research suppliers on their own before they make contact. They search Google, ask ChatGPT and other AI assistants, read LinkedIn and check supplier websites. EGGA makes sure that when they look, they find the client and understand it in seconds. The work covers SEO, AI search visibility (being named in answers from tools such as ChatGPT), content that answers buyers' real questions, website and offer pages, and campaigns aimed at the people who buy.

### 3. Customers: attention into conversations into closed work

Attention only counts when it becomes a conversation with someone who can sign. EGGA builds the path from first click to first meeting: lead generation aimed at buyers with budget, Google, Meta and LinkedIn campaigns, email nurture, follow-up and CRM that run on schedule, and sales materials the team actually uses. The client's sales team owns the deal. EGGA keeps the pipeline full and warm.

### 4. Scale: new markets, bigger accounts and monthly proof

Once the engine runs, EGGA points it at bigger prizes: a new market or a new language, a new offer for existing clients, larger accounts with better margins. Every month the client gets a plain-English report on what was done, what it moved and where the next dollar should go.

## How it works

1. **Brief.** The owner tells EGGA where the business is and where it should go, through the [contact page](https://egga.ai/contact). If there is a fit, EGGA sets up a free briefing call, looks at the business like an owner would, and shows what to fix first. The findings are the owner's to keep either way.
2. **Plan.** Month one is setup and strategy, included in the subscription: how the business makes money, the best-fit customer, a sharper offer and message, and the first 90-day plan.
3. **Execute.** Work runs on a shared deliverables calendar, with a dedicated group chat and a sync call twice a month.
4. **Grow and report.** A monthly report shows work shipped and progress against the goal, and the next 90 days are planned from what the numbers say.

## Plans and pricing model

EGGA sells one thing: a monthly subscription in three plans, **Base**, **Core** and **Full**. Every plan includes setup, a 90-day growth plan and access to any service. The plan sets the volume of work each month (focused, extended or intensive). Advertising budget stays on the client's own ad accounts and is paid directly to the platforms. Extra work is scoped and quoted in writing before it starts.

Prices are published in full rather than "on application". See [EGGA pricing in US dollars](https://egga.ai/pricing), [EGGA pricing for Singapore](https://egga.ai/sg/pricing) and [EGGA pricing for India](https://egga.ai/in/pricing).

## How EGGA works with clients

- **The business sets the plan.** EGGA starts from the company's history, team and constraints.
- **The truth comes first.** If the offer can be sharper or the website can sell harder, EGGA says so early.
- **Unfair means better prepared.** Sharper thinking, tighter systems and faster moves, played clean.
- **The client's team closes.** EGGA supplies leads, messaging and follow-up; the client's sales people own the deal.
- **Execution is the strategy.** A plan earns its keep in the offer, the message, the website and the follow-up.

## The name

EGGA comes from the Old Norse *eggja*, from *egg*, the edge of a blade. It is where English gets "egg on": to spur someone to go again and go further. EGGA gives SME owners an edge in markets that larger companies think they own.
